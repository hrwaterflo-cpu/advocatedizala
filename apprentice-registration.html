STEP 01

Portal Sign Up & Account Creation

Phase 1: Registration

Visit the official portal at apprenticeshipindia.gov.in. Click on Register in the top header and choose Candidate. Select your qualification type (ITI Passout / Fresher / Skill Certificate / PMKVY). Enter your full name exactly as written on your Aadhaar Card, father/mother's name, date of birth, gender, mobile number, and email. Set your password and submit. The portal generates your permanent Candidate Code (A-Number).

Important: Save your Candidate Code (A-Number) safely. This code is your permanent national identification across all training contracts.
Followed By Next Action: Proceed to Step 02: Email Activation
STEP 02

Email Verification & Account Activation

Phase 2: Activation

Check your registered email inbox for an automated activation message from the Apprenticeship Portal. Open the email and click the Activate button or URL link. Once confirmed, your candidate profile is activated and ready for login.

Followed By Next Action: Proceed to Step 03: Aadhaar e-KYC
STEP 03

Complete Mandatory Aadhaar e-KYC

Phase 3: Mandatory Authentication

Log in to your candidate dashboard using your Candidate Code or registered email. Click on Complete e-KYC. Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number and generate OTP. Enter the 6-digit OTP received on your mobile. Your profile photo, legal name, gender, date of birth, and permanent address are automatically verified and locked from UIDAI database records.

Statutory Rule: Under NAPS-2 guidelines, candidates who have not completed Aadhaar e-KYC are barred from applying for opportunities and signing training contracts.
Followed By Next Action: Proceed to Step 04: Profile & DBT Bank Linking
STEP 04

Profile Completion (100%) & DBT Bank Account Linking

Phase 4: Profile 100%

Complete all remaining data sections to bring your profile to 100%:

  • Educational Records: Add highest qualification (10th/12th/ITI/Diploma/Graduation), school/college name, marks percentage, and upload marksheets.
  • Bank Details for DBT: Input bank account number, account holder name, and IFSC code. Ensure your bank account is seeded with Aadhaar and mapped with NPCI for Direct Benefit Transfer.
  • Profile Verification: Verify that the dashboard shows 100% Completed.
Followed By Next Action: Proceed to Step 05: Apply for Opportunities
STEP 05

Search & Apply for Apprenticeship Opportunities

Phase 5: Job Application

Navigate to Apprenticeship Opportunities tab:

  • Course Filter: Designated Technical Trades (Fitter, Electrician, Welder, Turner, Machinist) or Optional Service Trades (HR, Accounts, Sales, Quality, Logistics).
  • Location Filter: Filter by State and District (e.g., Rajkot, Gujarat).
  • Review & Apply: Check monthly stipend, training duration, and company details. Click Apply.
Followed By Next Action: Proceed to Step 06: Digital Contract E-Signing
STEP 06

Offer Acceptance & Digital Contract E-Signing

Phase 6: Legal Contract

Upon selection by the employer, an official contract is issued to your dashboard. Review the contract in the Contracts section:

  • Verify training tenure (start date and end date) and weekly working hours.
  • Verify total stipend amount (Company share + Govt DBT subsidy share).
  • Click Sign Contract and authenticate with your Aadhaar OTP. The digitally executed contract is registered under the Apprentices Act.
Followed By Next Action: Proceed to Step 07: Training & DBT Stipend
STEP 07

Training Commencement, Monthly Attendance & DBT Stipend

Phase 7: Certification

Join the establishment on the designated start date:

  • Your company marks monthly attendance on the portal.
  • The company transfers its stipend share directly to your account.
  • The Government of India transfers its NAPS subsidy share (up to ₹1,500/month) directly into your Aadhaar-linked account via DBT.
  • Upon training completion, an assessment is conducted to issue your recognized National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC).
Final Milestone: Full Candidate Lifecycle Complete

STEP 01

Establishment Portal Registration & PAN Validation

Phase 1: Registration

Visit apprenticeshipindia.gov.in, click on Register and choose Establishment. Enter the exact company name matching the Income Tax PAN card. Provide company PAN (validated online in real-time), GSTIN, Establishment Type (Pvt Ltd, Public, LLP, Partnership, Prop, PSU), and Authorized Signatory contact details. Submit to receive your permanent Establishment Code (E-Number).

Followed By Next Action: Proceed to Step 02: Account Activation
STEP 02

Account Activation & Portal Login

Phase 2: Activation

Open the verification link sent to the official admin email. Once confirmed, log in to the Employer Dashboard using your E-Number and password credentials.

Followed By Next Action: Proceed to Step 03: Profile Setup & Quotas
STEP 03

Complete Establishment Profile, Locations & Quotas

Phase 3: Statutory Setup

Complete establishment compliance modules:

  • Operating Units: Add registered head office and operational factory, warehouse, and branch locations.
  • Manpower Declaration: Declare total strength of regular payroll employees and contractual staff (used to calculate your mandatory 2.5% to 15% quota under the Apprentices Act).
  • Statutory Codes & Bank: Enter PF code, ESIC code, LIN, and verified bank account for subsidy reconciliations.
  • Authority Approval: Submit profile for verification by the State Apprenticeship Advisor (SAA) or RDSDE.
Followed By Next Action: Proceed to Step 04: Post Opportunities
STEP 04

Trade Selection & Opportunity Posting

Phase 4: Openings

Navigate to Opportunities > Create Opportunity:

  • Designated Trades: Engineering/technical trades (e.g., Fitter, Electrician, Machinist, Welder).
  • Optional Trades: Commercial/modern roles (e.g., HR Assistant, Accounting Executive, Back Office, Quality Inspector, Retail, Logistics).
  • Define total vacancy slots, required educational qualification, duration (e.g. 12 months), and monthly stipend. Publish the vacancy.
Followed By Next Action: Proceed to Step 05: Screen & Issue Contracts
STEP 05

Screen Applicants & Issue Apprenticeship Contracts

Phase 5: Contract Generation

Review received candidate applications or search the candidate pool. Shortlist qualified candidates and click Issue Contract. Set training commencement and completion dates, daily work timing, and stipend split (Company share + Govt subsidy share). Submit contract offer to candidate.

Followed By Next Action: Proceed to Step 06: Regulatory Approval
STEP 06

Candidate E-Sign & Regulatory Approval (SAA / RDSDE)

Phase 6: Execution

The candidate e-signs the contract via Aadhaar OTP on their dashboard. Once signed, the contract forwards to the State Apprenticeship Advisor (SAA) or RDSDE for statutory verification (several optional trades feature immediate auto-approval).

Followed By Next Action: Proceed to Step 07: Monthly Attendance & DBT Payroll
STEP 07

Monthly Attendance, Payroll & NAPS-2 DBT Reconciliation

Phase 7: Payroll & Compliance

Maintain statutory operations throughout the training tenure:

  • Monthly Portal Attendance: Record apprentice attendance by the monthly portal cut-off date.
  • Stipend Disbursement: Pay the establishment's stipend share via portal payment gateway or bank transfer.
  • NAPS-2 DBT Transfer: Government transfers its subsidy share (up to ₹1,500/month or 25%) directly into the candidate's account via DBT.
  • Final Assessment: Coordinate final trade assessments for candidate certification.

Statutory Mandate

Mandatory Apprenticeship Quota (Apprentices Act, 1961)

Under Section 8 of the Apprentices Act, 1961, every establishment must fulfill the prescribed apprenticeship quota:

30 or More Total Employees: MANDATORY to engage apprentices between 2.5% to 15% of total manpower (regular payroll + contractual).
4 to 29 Employees: VOLUNTARY (Optional). Establishments can engage apprentices to avail government subsidies.
Less than 4 Employees: NOT permitted to engage apprentices under the Act.
Corporate Cost Savings

Significant Statutory & Financial Benefits

  • 100% PF (EPF) Exemption: Apprentices are legally defined as trainees under Section 18 of the Apprentices Act, not regular employees. No PF contribution is required.
  • 100% ESIC Exemption: Establishments are not required to pay ESI contributions for apprentices, lowering compliance overhead.
  • Government NAPS-2 DBT Subsidy: Government reimburses 25% of prescribed stipend up to ₹1,500/month per apprentice directly to the candidate's account via DBT.
  • Zero Retrenchment / Notice Pay Liabilities: Contract automatically terminates upon training completion without statutory retrenchment compensation liabilities.
