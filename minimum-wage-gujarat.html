GUJARAT MINIMUM WAGE UPDATE

Effective from 01st October 2026 to 31st March 2027
As per the latest notification, the Special Allowance / VDA has been revised from ₹60.50 to ₹72.50 per day for all categories under the Factory Act (Manufacturing).

Key Highlights
  • Special Allowance / VDA increased from ₹60.50 to ₹72.50 per day.
  • All categories (Skilled, Semi-Skilled, Unskilled) get ₹12 per day increase.
  • Monthly increase is ₹312 (calculated on standard 26 working days).
  • Basic wage remains unchanged. (Only Special Allowance / VDA has increased).
Zone I & Zone II – What's the Difference?
  • Zone I generally applies to areas within Rajkot Municipal Corporation limits and other major municipal corporation jurisdictions.
  • Zone II applies to industrial areas, GIDC estates, and establishments outside municipal corporation limits.
  • Please confirm the exact zone applicable to your factory / unit location as per the latest government gazette notification.
How Minimum Wage is Calculated?
Basic Wage + Special Allowance (VDA) = Minimum Wage
Example (Skilled – Zone I):
• Basic Wage ₹474.00
• Special Allowance (VDA) ₹72.50
• Total per Day ₹546.50
• Monthly (26 days) ₹14,209.00
Important Notes
  • These statutory rates are applicable only for the Factory / Manufacturing process under the Factories Act.
  • Ensure employee's actual gross wages are not less than the statutory minimum wage applicable for their category and zone.
  • This is not a straightforward "Basic Salary" amount. Salary structure, PF (12%), ESIC (3.25%), bonus and allowances should be structured as per compliance rules.
  • Default in minimum wage payment attracts penalty and legal action under the Minimum Wages Act, 1948.
